enum class OurPaintDCM::Utils::RequirementType : uint8_t
strong

Enum which illustrates every requirement type Each value describes a geometric constraint that can be applied to points, sections (lines), circles, or arcs in the system. These constraints are used by solvers to maintain relationships between geometric objects.

Enumerator
ET_POINTLINEDIST 

Fixed distance between point and section.

ET_POINTONLINE 

Point must lie on a section (distance == 0).

ET_POINTPOINTDIST 

Fixed distance between two points.

ET_POINTONPOINT 

Two points must coincide(distance == 0).

ET_LINECIRCLEDIST 

Fixed distance between section and circle.

ET_LINEONCIRCLE 

Section endpoints must be on a circle.

ET_LINEINCIRCLE 

Section lies entirely inside a circle.

ET_LINELINEPARALLEL 

Two sections must be parallel.

ET_LINELINEPERPENDICULAR 

Two sections must be perpendicular.

ET_LINELINEANGLE 

Fixed angle between two sections.

ET_VERTICAL 

Section must be vertical (along Y-axis).

ET_HORIZONTAL 

Section must be horizontal (along X-axis).

ET_ARCCENTERONPERPENDICULAR 

Default constraints for every arc, for making 3 points working like a arc.

ET_FIXPOINT 

Fix a point at its current position.

ET_FIXLINE 

Fix a line (both endpoints) at its current position.

ET_FIXCIRCLE 

Fix a circle (center and radius) at its current position.

ET_POINTONCIRCLE 

Point lies on a circle.

ET_CIRCLERADIUS 

Circle has a prescribed radius; its center remains free.

ET_CIRCLEDIAMETER 

Circle has a prescribed diameter; its center remains free.

ET_EQUALLENGTH 

Two sections have equal lengths, including zero lengths.

ET_EQUALRADIUS 

Two circles have equal positive radii.

ET_POINTATMIDPOINT 

Point is the midpoint of a section (two independent scalar equations).
